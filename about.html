About MRR Technology Solutions

A founder-led marine mobility company building sustainable luxury tourism from Karnataka's coastline — and India's first indigenous hybrid solar yacht platform.

Our Story

Rooted in Karnataka's Waters

MRR Technology Solutions Private Limited was founded in 2019 on Karnataka's coastline, built on years of hands-on marine tourism operations, premium yacht charters and an intimate understanding of coastal routes from Mangalore to Karwar. That operational foundation is now the launchpad for something bigger: an indigenous, sustainable yacht platform engineered for India's waters.

Today, MRR runs premium charter experiences along the Karnataka coast, with reach extending to Goa. Tomorrow, that same operational insight will power Project Blue Horizon — India's first indigenous hybrid solar yacht platform, designed, engineered and commercialised from Karnataka.
