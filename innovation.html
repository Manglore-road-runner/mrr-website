The Problem

India Still Imports Its Luxury Yachts

India's premium marine tourism market depends heavily on imported, second-hand or refurbished yachts. Most existing vessels use diesel propulsion, leading to higher operating cost, emissions, noise and limited sustainability positioning — despite India having over 7,500 km of coastline and 1,300+ islands.

Karnataka's coastline — Mangalore, Udupi and Karwar — is exactly the kind of emerging destination that lacks structured, premium and sustainable marine tourism infrastructure. Project Blue Horizon starts here.

The Innovation

An 8-Seater Premium Demonstrator

Centred on a lightweight composite, electric-propulsion demonstrator, designed for luxury tourism, inland waterways and future marine mobility applications.
